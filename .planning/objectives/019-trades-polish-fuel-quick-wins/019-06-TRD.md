  return '${m}m';

<anti_patterns>
- **Do NOT compute optimization inside the widget.** The widget renders the diff. Consumer provides both `before` and `after` route lists.
- **Do NOT round mileage/time values until display.** Store as raw doubles / Duration; format only at the EdenKpiTile.value string.
- **Do NOT auto-accept the optimization.** Even when metrics show clear gain (15% fewer miles), require explicit `onAccept` callback to fire.
- **Do NOT mutate the inbound lists.** Treat as immutable.
- **Do NOT show negative-improvement deltas with negative-polarity = bad.** Miles increasing IS bad (polarity=negativeIsGood means a NEGATIVE delta is GOOD; positive delta is BAD = red). Verify the EdenKpiTrendPolarity semantic at TRD-execute by inspecting obj 012-02 usage.
</anti_patterns>
